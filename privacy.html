Privacy Policy – Phone Metrics

Last updated: 28 December 2025

Overview

Phone Metrics is a simple utility application that displays battery information, RAM usage, and an estimated frame‑per‑second (FPS) value on your device. This app is designed to respect your privacy and does not collect or sell personal data.

Information We Collect

Phone Metrics does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). The app does not require you to create an account or provide any personal details.

Device Data Used Locally

To display metrics, the app reads limited, non‑personal system information directly from your device:

  • Battery level and charging status.
  • Memory information, such as total and free RAM.
  • Rendering performance indicators used to estimate FPS.

This information is used only to show the metrics on your screen. It is processed locally on the device and is not uploaded to any server.

Third‑Party Services

Phone Metrics does not integrate analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or other third‑party tracking services. No behavioral or usage profiles are created.

Data Sharing and Selling

The app does not share, sell, or rent any user data to third parties.

Children’s Privacy

Phone Metrics is a general utility app and does not target children specifically. Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Security

Since Phone Metrics does not transmit or store your personal data on external servers, the risk of data exposure from server‑side breaches is minimized. You should still keep your device and operating system updated for overall security.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app’s behavior changes (for example, if new features require additional data). Any significant changes will be reflected on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how the app handles data, please contact the developer using the support options provided in the Indus Appstore listing.
